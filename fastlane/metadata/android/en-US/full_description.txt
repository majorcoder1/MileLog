MileLog keeps your business miles, expenses and income on your phone, with no account, no server and no subscription.

- Tracks drives by GPS, odometer readings, or typed-in miles
- Detects driving automatically by watching the phone's own GPS, with working-hours scheduling
- Swipe right for work, left for personal
- Expenses and revenue with receipt photos, and your profit worked out
- Handles the IRS mileage rate correctly, including mid-year changes
- Builds a year-end spreadsheet for your tax preparer
- Optional backup to a folder you choose

Your trips, including their GPS routes, never leave the phone.
