# Privacy policy — MileLog

MileLog is published by 4The People Media (Aaron McCreery, Crossville, Tennessee, USA).

- **Everything stays on your phone.** Trips (including their GPS routes), expenses, revenue, receipt photos and settings are stored in a database on your device. MileLog has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it does not sell or share data.
- **Location.** MileLog uses your location, including in the background when you turn on automatic drive detection, only to record the miles you drive. Background location is needed so a drive can be recorded while the app is closed.
- **Physical activity.** In the Google Play build, the optional "Google motion detection" uses Google's activity-recognition service to notice that you started driving. You can switch to the phone-GPS method instead.
- **Network use.** Map tiles for viewing a trip are loaded from OpenStreetMap, which sees your IP address and the map area you view.
- **Backups and exports.** Year-end spreadsheets and backups are created only when you ask, and go to the mail app or folder you choose.
- **Children.** MileLog is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
